About Global Scholarship Network

Connecting Students With Global Opportunities

Global Scholarship Network is a platform dedicated to helping students discover scholarships, fellowships, internships, conferences and other international opportunities.

Who We Are

Global Scholarship Network is built to make international educational and professional opportunities easier to discover.

Students often have to search through many different websites and platforms to find relevant opportunities. Our goal is to bring useful opportunities together in one accessible place.

We aim to provide students with clear information so they can explore opportunities and make informed decisions about their academic and professional future.
